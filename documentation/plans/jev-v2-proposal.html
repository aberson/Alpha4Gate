What I heard

Keep the four-Gateway identity, then develop a player that scouts, expands and adapts against harder opponents. Jev owns the whole decision process: strategic choices, execution choices and the graph that carries them through. Make those decisions visible live. The test must open the correct dashboard game before SC2 starts; no menu navigation should be necessary.

Why this shape

The current executor can only build Pylons and Gateways and train Probes and Zealots. V2 needs real gas, expansion and tech actions before the hosted model can usefully choose them. We will measure baseline weaknesses first and preserve its executable source.

Not in this milestone

No automatic evolution, runtime graph rewriting, full tech tree, spells, research upgrades, cloak detection, more than two bases or embedded SC2 window. Scope is Simple64 against built-in opponents.

Risks

More complexity may not win more games. Reservations may starve production; model answers may arrive late; a small test panel may overstate strength. Deadlines, explicit fallbacks, immutable provenance and held-out comparison make these failures visible.

Hosted match cap: 450 calls; batch cap: six matches, two hours and 2,700 calls. Tokens are reported; these are not currency guarantees. No paid matches run during planning.
