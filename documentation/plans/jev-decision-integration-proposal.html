Accepted design defaults D

D1 · Safety wins locally

Emergency defense preempts intent; exclude empty-army recovery; fall back in the same tick when an answer becomes infeasible.

Why: a syntactically valid model answer must not break execution.

Tweak: threat predicates and capability-specific fallback; retreat/trade remains excluded.

D2 · Existing capabilities first

Use current units, tech and expansion branches; compare complete production scenarios with explicit unknowns.

Why: changing the army's capabilities at the same time would blur what the model contributed.

Tweak: first-milestone breadth; new units/build orders can be a separate next phase.

D3 · Prepare at three Zealots

Ask ahead of the four-unit launch. Allow a single, nonblocking wait of at most two game seconds; revalidate decision meaning rather than every unit tag.

Why: make the first answer timely without delaying emergency reactions.

Tweak: preparation trigger and wait budget, measured offline before live play.

D4 · Thirty seconds of context

Include visible strength proxies, recent own losses, trends and conservatively labeled last-fight observations.

Why: a single snapshot hides direction of change; fog-of-war uncertainty must remain visible.

Tweak: history horizon and proxy detail; no invented combat odds.

D5 · Independent macro priority

Let the model choose feasible tech, expansion or a bounded ten-second hold independently of army pressure/recovery.

Why: recovery should not accidentally veto macro development.

Tweak: hold duration and next strategic choices; preserve local prerequisites.

D6 · Four arms, same graph

Scripted, fixed Stalker-heavy scripted, current-question hosted, improved-question hosted. Eighteen matched cases per arm.

Why: distinguish useful model decisions from integration fixes and constant defaults.

Tweak: panel size and screening rule: two extra wins versus each scripted control, with no race regression or safety failure.

D7 · Stage the spending

Working schedule: 15 games including smokes, eight hosted; then 60 remaining games, 30 hosted. Allocate spending across stages as needed under one approved $5 cumulative maximum. There is no $1 stage-one subcap or second-stage permission requirement.
